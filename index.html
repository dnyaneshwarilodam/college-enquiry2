About Us

A Legacy of Excellence Since 1985

Greenfield College of Arts, Science & Technology has been a cornerstone of quality education for nearly four decades. Spread across a lush 50-acre campus, we offer undergraduate and postgraduate programs designed to blend academic rigour with real-world skills.

Our NAAC 'A' accreditation reflects our commitment to continuous improvement in teaching, research, and student support. With over 15,000 alumni working across the globe, Greenfield is where ambition meets opportunity.
