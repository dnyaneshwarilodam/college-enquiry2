About Us

A Legacy of Quality Technical Education

Dr. Rajendra Gode Institute of Technology & Research (DRGITR), Amravati is a premier technical institution committed to nurturing skilled engineers and technicians. Situated in a scenic campus on University-Mardi Road, Amravati, our institute provides quality diploma, undergraduate, and professional engineering education.

With advanced laboratories, seasoned faculty members, active industry partnerships, and dedicated training programs, DRGITR prepares students for leadership in competitive technology domains across India and globally.

  • Approved by AICTE New Delhi, DTE Maharashtra & Affiliated to SGBAU / MSBTE
  • State-of-the-art computer labs, mechanical workshops & electronic testing suites
  • Dedicated Training, Placement & Career Guidance Cell
  • Comprehensive technical education with industry workshops & internships
